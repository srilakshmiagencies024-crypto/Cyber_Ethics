CYBER ETHICS AND ONLINE RESPONSIBILITY

Cyber Ethics and Online Responsibility

Be honest, kind, and respectful online. Think before you post and stand up against cyberbullying.

Cybercrimes and Digital Safety

  • Use strong passwords and keep them private.
  • Do not share personal details with strangers.
  • Never misuse your computer skills to harm others.

Digital Ownership and Intellectual Property

  • Give credit to creators when using their work.
  • Use free and open-source resources.
  • Do not copy or share without permission.

Ethical Use of AI

  • Use AI tools to learn, not to cheat.
  • Check the accuracy of AI-generated information.
  • Be aware of how your data is collected and used.
